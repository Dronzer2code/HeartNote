# Heartnote demo video script (under 2 minutes)

Narration (VO) for the demo video. The voice message recorded on screen in scene 4 is the separate "message script" (We can send voice messages to anyone…). The VO goes quiet while that message plays.

Total ≈ 1:55. Speak calmly; the pauses carry the feeling.

---

## 1. Opening — 0:00–0:10

**On screen:** Black. The Heartnote logo fades in, beating gently. Cut to a finger tapping the app icon.

**VO:**
> This is Heartnote — voice notes that carry your real heartbeat.

---

## 2. Presage SmartSpectra — 0:10–0:28

**On screen:** The Record screen, the front camera showing a face inside the red particle sphere.

**VO:**
> Heartnote is built on Presage SmartSpectra. With nothing but the phone's front camera, SmartSpectra reads your pulse and your breathing from tiny changes in your face, as you talk.
> No smartwatch. No sensor. Nothing to wear or touch. Just look at your phone and speak.

---

## 3. Privacy first — 0:28–0:38

**On screen:** First launch: the consent screen ("Your heartbeat stays yours."), then tap "I understand".

**VO:**
> And it's yours. Heartnote never saves the camera video, keeps your notes on your phone, and lets you delete any note at any time.

---

## 4. Recording — 0:38–1:05

**On screen:** Tick "I am recording myself" → Start → "Finding your pulse…" → the live pulse number appears in the sphere, the breathing line moves, and the sphere beats in time. Then the speaker records the message.

**VO (before the message):**
> Tap Start. In a few seconds it finds your pulse, and the sphere starts beating with your heart. Then you just say what you want to say.

**On screen:** The speaker's message plays out (sped up or trimmed to about 15 s). Captions show the words.

*(No VO here. Let the message speak.)*

---

## 5. Playback — 1:05–1:25

**On screen:** The saved note opens and plays. The dot rows fill as it plays. A **second camera** films the phone on a table or in a hand, vibrating on each beat.

**VO:**
> When it's played back, your voice comes with your heartbeat. The beats Heartnote measured become a soft heartbeat sound under your voice, and the phone vibrates on every beat — the same rhythm, at the same moments.

---

## 6. Hold — 1:25–1:35

**On screen:** Tap the hand-heart button. The phone lies in an open palm, the particle heart beating on a dark red screen.

**VO:**
> Or just hold it. Put the phone in your hand and feel their heart beat in your palm.

---

## 7. Share — 1:35–1:50

**On screen:** Share sheet → "Share video" → WhatsApp. Cut to a second phone: the video plays. Then the file opens in Heartnote, and the phone vibrates.

**VO:**
> Send it to anyone. It goes as a video that plays anywhere, and when it's opened in Heartnote, the heartbeat and the vibration come with it.

---

## 8. Close — 1:50–1:57

**On screen:** Back to black. The logo beats once, then the tagline appears.

**VO:**
> Heartnote. Don't just hear the moment. Feel it.

**Text on screen:** *Heartnote — built with Presage SmartSpectra.*

---

## Filming tips

- **Light:** face a window or a soft lamp; avoid light from behind. Turn Battery saver off and let the phone cool before recording; it starts much faster.
- **Vibration:** the vibration only shows on a second camera. Film the phone close up, on a table or in a palm, and add the heartbeat sound so viewers can feel the timing.
- **Captions:** add captions for the VO and the message; many people watch muted.
- **Wording:** keep to "pulse", "heartbeat" and "breathing". Don't call it a health, medical or monitoring app.
